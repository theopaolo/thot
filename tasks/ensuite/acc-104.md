## Product rules

A teacher creates an enrolment link for one or more of their courses and shares it, in the
school's ENT for example. A learner who opens it joins those courses. A learner without an account
creates one on the same page with a name, an identifier and a password. A class of 200 then signs
up without the teacher typing 200 rows.

A link has an expiry and a number of places, both set by the teacher, 14 days and 40 places by
default. The teacher sees the links of their courses with their remaining places and revokes them.
An expired, revoked or full link enrols no one and creates no account. Revoking a link keeps the
learners it enrolled. The token is random, 128 bits, and the URL names no account.

The link enrols learners only. A teacher or administrator session that opens it gets an
explanation and no membership. Teacher membership stays with administrators. A personal instance
has no links.

A learner who signs up through a link chooses their own password, so the account does not ask for
a new one at the first log-in. A learner with an account logs in first and comes back to the link.

## Privacy

[The family notice](../../docs/notice-familles.md) states that the school creates each account.
With links, learners type their own name and identifier. Update the notice and
[operations](../../docs/exploitation.md) in the same commit. Teachers see who joined in the course
lists of ACC-103 and remove the wrong accounts.

## Backend

- [x] Store links with their token, creator, courses, expiry, places and revocation, in a new
  migration.
- [x] `GET /inscription/:token` shows the courses, then the join button for a logged-in learner or
  the sign-up form with a log-in link for a visitor. `POST` enrols the account, or creates and
  enrols it, in one transaction that also takes the place, then opens the session.
- [x] Log-in returns to the link afterwards. The return address accepts `/inscription/` paths
  only.
- [x] Refuse a link for a course its creator does not belong to.

## Interface

`/prof/eleves` gets a section to create a link (ticked courses, expiry, places), copy its URL, and
list and revoke active links. The join page names the courses and the teacher who created the
link. A QR code waits until a teacher asks to project the link in class.

Point the refusal of an identifier already in use (`creerEleves`) and the hint under the
identifier field of `/prof/eleves` to the link. Both send the learner to an administrator today.

## Acceptance

- [x] A visitor with a valid link creates an account, lands on `/eleve` and reads the course's
  published sources.
- [x] A learner from another course joins with one click and keeps their other courses.
- [x] An expired, revoked or full link creates no account and enrols no one.
- [x] A teacher session that opens a link gets no membership. A teacher creates links for their own
  courses only.
- [x] The log-in return address refuses anything outside `/inscription/`.
