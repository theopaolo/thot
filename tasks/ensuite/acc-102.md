## Product rules

A course has teacher and learner members. Membership bounds what [ACC-101](acc-101.md) calls
colleagues and learners. A source shared with colleagues reaches the teachers of its courses, and
a published source reaches the learners of its courses. A source with no course keeps the
instance-wide audience. The learners of a course form its learner group. Add a separate cohort
record only when one course serves classes that need different publications. Sharing with a
named colleague waits for a request that needs it.

## Backend

- [x] Store course membership for teacher and learner accounts. Administrators manage it in the
  subjects and courses page and in the CLI. [CFG-101](cfg-101.md) later moves it into settings.
- [x] Let the learner CSV import at `/prof/eleves` enrol the new accounts in courses chosen by the
  importing teacher, preselecting that teacher's courses. A non-administrator teacher can then
  bring a class in without waiting for an administrator.
- [x] Show a teacher only their own courses in the upload and affiliation forms. Administrators see
  every course.
- [x] Add membership to the ACC-101 access check. Removing a member revokes access through that
  check, including the evidence recheck of earlier answers. A call with an explicit `null` reader
  (worker suggestion generation, CLI evaluations) keeps the instance-wide learner view.
- [x] Filter suggested questions and the home page fact by the reader, since a learner outside a
  course must not see questions drawn from its sources.
- [x] Let the teachers of a course edit and review its sources, alongside the owner and
  administrators.
- [x] Show a teacher, in `/prof/questions`, only the exchanges scoped to their courses. An unscoped
  exchange goes to the teachers who share a course with the learner, or to every teacher when the
  learner has no course. Administrators read every exchange. Tell learners who can read their
  questions before they ask.
- [x] Enrol every existing teacher and learner in every existing course during migration, so pilot
  access stays the same. A personal instance needs no enrolment: its owner reaches all its courses.

## Interface

List members under each course in the subjects and courses page, with add and remove. Learners see
the courses they belong to. The full settings layout belongs to CFG-101.

- [x] Tests add an unrelated teacher and an unrelated learner to the ACC-101 cases. Neither reads,

## Delivered

Table `membres_cours` holds course membership. Migration 0009 enrols every existing account in
every existing course, so the pilot reads what it read before. `FILTRE_ACCES` adds membership:
a shared or published source with courses is read by the members of one of them, a source without
a course keeps the instance-wide audience. Owners and administrators keep their access. A call
with an explicit `null` reader, used by the worker and the CLI evaluations, sees what is published
to learners.

Teachers of a course edit, review and bulk-review its sources that are not private. A teacher
affiliates sources only to their own courses, and edits keep the links to other courses. An
affiliated source keeps at least one course, because removing the last one would open it to the
whole instance. An upload without a ticked course goes to the first course of its subject that the
teacher belongs to, or stays unclassified. Only the CLI and administrators create a course that
way. The upload and source forms, the library filter, learner filters and the learner home name
only the reader's courses, and a learner asks inside a course only as its member.

Suggested questions, the home page fact and answer follow-ups follow the reader. `/prof/questions`
shows a teacher the exchanges of their courses, and unscoped ones from learners who share a course
with them or have none. A stored séquence name appears only when the teacher reads a source in it.
A teacher lists and resets passwords only for learners who share one of their courses. Only
administrators manage learners with no course, so a reset cannot open an unrelated account.

Administrators list, add and remove members under each course, and the CLI has `membre` and
`retirer-membre`. The learner CSV import enrols the new accounts in the teacher's ticked courses
and refuses other courses. Learners see who reads their questions next to every question form.

## Verification

- `deno task check`: 100 passed, no failures.
- `tests/acces_test.ts` covers two courses with their teachers and learners: reads, images,
  search, suggestions, edits, bulk review, refused affiliation, the learner notice, a learner
  removed during generation, question review scoping and migration 0009.
- `tests/domaines_test.ts` covers the members page and its 403 for non-administrators, the CSV
  enrolment and its refusal, and the CLI commands.
- Twenty mutations each make a test fail: the membership clause, matching any course, a course
  teacher touching a private source, writes without membership, foreign affiliations, learners of
  other courses in the list and resets, filing outside the teacher's courses, removing the last
  course, dropping foreign links, foreign séquence and course names, questions in a foreign course,
  suggestions and the home fact without the reader, always-active library checkboxes, and the
  course, topic, subject and unscoped question rules.
- The members list, the import page and the learner notice were checked in the browser on desktop
  and at 375 px.
- An independent review found a learner-account takeover through password resets, filing paths
  that escaped a teacher's courses, the last-course removal that opened a source to everyone, and
  course and séquence names shown outside their courses. Each is fixed and has a regression test.

A course created later starts empty. That includes the course `rangerLegacy` creates when a CLI
import names a subject that has none. Its resources stay with their owner and administrators until
someone enrols the readers.
