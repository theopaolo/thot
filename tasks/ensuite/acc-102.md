## Product rules

A course has teacher and learner members. Membership bounds what [ACC-101](acc-101.md) calls
colleagues and learners. A source shared with colleagues reaches the teachers of its courses, and
a published source reaches the learners of its courses. A source with no course keeps the
instance-wide audience. The learners of a course form its learner group. Add a separate cohort
record only when one course serves classes that need different publications. Sharing with a
named colleague waits for a request that needs it.

## Backend

- [ ] Store course membership for teacher and learner accounts. Administrators manage it in the
  subjects and courses page and in the CLI. [CFG-101](cfg-101.md) later moves it into settings.
- [ ] Add membership to the ACC-101 access check. Removing a member revokes access through that
  check, including the evidence recheck of earlier answers.
- [ ] Let the teachers of a course edit and review its sources, alongside the owner and
  administrators.
- [ ] Show a teacher, in `/prof/questions`, only the exchanges scoped to their courses or citing
  their courses' sources. Unscoped exchanges without citations go to administrators. Tell learners
  who can read their questions before they ask.
- [ ] Enrol every existing teacher and learner in every existing course during migration, so pilot
  access stays the same. A personal instance needs no enrolment: its owner reaches all its courses.

## Interface

List members under each course in the subjects and courses page, with add and remove. Learners see
the courses they belong to. The full settings layout belongs to CFG-101.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Tests add an unrelated teacher and an unrelated learner to the ACC-101 cases. Neither reads,
  retrieves or cites a source from a course they do not belong to.
- [ ] Removing a learner from a course during generation keeps that course's text out of the
  answer.
- [ ] A teacher sees only the learner questions of their courses.
- [ ] Every pilot account reads the same sources after migration as before.
