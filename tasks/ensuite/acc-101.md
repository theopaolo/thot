## Product rules

Use a small fixed set of capabilities: administer the instance, manage teaching materials,
review/publish them, and learn from accessible materials. One account may combine these
capabilities. Avoid a user-defined role builder.

A source is private to its owner, shared with colleagues, or published to learners. In this task,
colleagues are the instance's teachers and learners are its learners. [ACC-102](acc-102.md)
narrows both to course members. A source's extraction/review state and its eligibility as answer
evidence are independent from its audience. Personal selection is labelled as such and does not
imply expert certification. A course affiliation alone does not widen the audience.

## Backend

- [x] Associate each source with its owner's account identifier. Map the stored teacher name to an
  account only when exactly one teacher account has that name. Otherwise leave the owner empty,
  keep the name as historical attribution, and let administrators manage the source.
- [x] Store audience and answer eligibility in their own columns. Migrate `publics` without
  widening or narrowing any read: `eleves` publishes to learners, every other source stays shared
  with colleagues because every teacher reads it today, and `thot` sets answer eligibility.
- [x] Decide in one function whether an account can read a source and whether the source can
  support an answer for that account. Search, readers, originals, images, suggestions and
  citations use it. Core code receives the decision as a parameter, the way `parametresDomaine`
  passes the scope. Transcripts and media ranges use it once MED-101 and MED-102 add them.
- [x] Let reviewed teacher-only sources support that teacher's searches and answers. A source can
  be readable without being eligible as evidence.
- [x] Make the evidence recheck before publication and on each re-read (rule 1 of
  [architecture](../../docs/architecture.md)) call this check for the reader. A source made
  private during generation then makes the answer unavailable to a reader who lost access.
- [x] Keep conversations private to their account. Sharing or publishing a source does not share
  its owner's conversations or teacher-only answer keys.
- [x] Limit edits, review, re-extraction, audience changes and bulk review to the owner and
  administrators. [ACC-102](acc-102.md) adds the teachers of the source's courses.

## Interface

Use a compact audience control on resources and bulk review, with three values: available for my
study, shared with colleagues, published to learners. Show who reviewed the resource. The reviewed
revision appears once [ING-006](ing-006.md) stores revisions. Do not use qualification language
for source review. Add the shipped labels to [vocabulary](../../docs/vocabulaire.md).

## Delivered

Each source has an owner account, an audience and an answer-eligibility flag. Migration 0008 maps
the old `publics` values and removes them from the metadata. A source published to learners stays
published, every other source is shared with colleagues, and `thot` becomes the flag. The stored
teacher name designates the owner when exactly one teacher account has it, and CLI imports follow
the same rule. Ownerless sources are managed by administrators. The unused "Consultants
extérieurs" value is dropped: no account type reads it.

`FILTRE_ACCES` in `src/core/recherche.ts` is the read rule. Search, the evidence recheck, learner
pages, previews, images, the teacher library, source details, original PDFs, course filters and
the séquence labels of `/prof/questions` apply it with the reading account. Learner pages still
require review. Answers still require the flag, library search does not. Administrators read
everything. Edits, review, caption review, bulk review and bulk audience changes are limited to
the owner and administrators. Other teachers get a read-only source page.

The upload form, the source page and the library selection bar carry the audience control. The
library marks sources that are not published to learners.
