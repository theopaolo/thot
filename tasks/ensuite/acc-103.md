## Product rules

A teacher manages the learners of their own courses from the app. Administrators keep managing
teachers and every course. A teacher adds learners by hand or by CSV, and removes a learner from
one of their courses. Removal revokes access through the ACC-102 check. The account stays.

Adding by hand creates a learner account and enrols it in the ticked courses, as the CSV import
does for a class. A teacher does not enrol an existing account by its identifier. Membership lets
the course's teachers reset the learner's password, so enrolling an existing account by
identifier would let any teacher take over any learner account. The ACC-102 review closed that
takeover. An existing learner joins through the link of [ACC-104](acc-104.md), which they open
themselves, or through an administrator.

`thot compte` enrols the account it creates in the courses given with `--cours`, for operator
and test accounts.

## Backend

- [x] Add one learner at `/prof/eleves`: identifier, name, and the courses the teacher ticks among
  their own. Show the generated password once, as the CSV import does. Refuse an identifier
  already in use and tell the teacher how that learner can join.
- [x] Remove a learner from one of the teacher's courses. Refuse a course the teacher does not
  belong to.
- [x] `thot compte <identifiant> <nom> <enseignant|eleve> [--cours=<id>]...` enrols the new
  account. An unknown course id fails before the account exists.

## Interface

`/prof/eleves` lists the learners of each of the teacher's courses, with a remove button per row,
the add form and the CSV import. Administrators see every course. Learners with no course appear
in their own group, visible and managed only by administrators.

## Acceptance

- [x] A teacher adds a learner to their course. The learner logs in and reads that course's
  published sources.
- [x] A teacher cannot add or remove members of a course they do not belong to, and cannot add an
  identifier already in use.
- [x] A removed learner loses the course's sources on the next request. After removal from their
  last course, only administrators can list or reset their account.
- [x] `thot compte --cours` enrols the account. An unknown course creates no account.
