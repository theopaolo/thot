## Notes

Une réingestion réécrit `document.txt` en place. Les offsets des citations déjà données aux
élèves pointent alors vers un autre texte ([[ing-005]] le provoquera). Écrire chaque
extraction dans une révision et garder la révision citée par un ancien message.

## Extension for EVO-001

This is the shared prerequisite for richer extraction, corrected transcripts and durable citations.
Keep one implementation of revisions for [DOCX](ing-109.md), [PPTX](ing-110.md),
[media](med-101.md) and [structured answers](ans-101.md).

### Work

- [ ] Store an immutable extraction revision with canonical text, blocks, images/asset manifest,
  parser provenance and extraction warnings. A source identifies the resource, while its selected
  revision identifies the content currently available for new answers.
- [ ] Extend locators to pages, slides, paragraphs/table cells and time intervals as required by
  each format. Preserve the existing Unicode citation invariant. Rendering must not reconstruct
  offsets from differently formatted display text.
- [ ] Associate images with their real blocks/slides and distinguish embedded captions, generated
  descriptions and reviewed descriptions. Do not attach every image to the first text chunk.
- [ ] Pin citations, reviewed generated descriptions and learning activities to their evidence
  revision. Keep legacy responses readable by assigning their original canonical content a revision.
- [ ] Write new extraction output into a staging revision, verify it and then select it atomically.
  Review does not carry over silently when the evidence text or generated descriptions change.
- [ ] Keep old citations readable only while current access permits it. A retained revision is
  not a way around revocation, deletion or retention rules. Integrate lifecycle work with SRC-002.

### Acceptance

- [ ] Re-extracting changed text or correcting a transcript leaves old citations at their original
  passage and location.
- [ ] A failed extraction or index write leaves the previous usable revision intact.
- [ ] Image, table, slide and timestamp references survive reindexing and process restart.
- [ ] Access revocation and deletion affect historical revisions and derived responses.
