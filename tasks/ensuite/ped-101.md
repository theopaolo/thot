## Outcome

A teacher or trainer selects an objective and source materials, prepares an explanation or
practice activity, reviews it and publishes it to the intended learners. A personal learner can
use the same preparation tools privately, with generated content identified as such.

## Backend

- [x] Introduce learning objectives associated with courses and optional competency/exam references.
  Preserve the referenced framework's version. Do not infer that a document covers a competency
  without a reviewable link.
- [ ] Start with a study explanation and a short-answer exercise containing a prompt, expected
  answer or rubric, source references and feedback. Reuse the source-backed fields envisaged in
  [FIC-101](fic-101.md), [FIC-102](fic-102.md) and [REVI-101](revi-101.md) rather than implementing
  a competing generation pipeline.
- [x] Store generated resources as drafts with source revisions, author, review history and an
  intended audience. Human edits invalidate the relevant prior approval until reviewed again.
- [x] Keep exercise prompts and answer keys under separate response/access rules. The expected
  answer must not be delivered in page HTML or a browser data attribute before disclosure.
- [x] Associate practice attempts with the learner, activity and activity revision. Give feedback
  appropriate to the rubric. Avoid universal substring matching as a correctness rule.
- [ ] Support a practical procedure and a case-analysis rubric after the first activity passes
  domain evaluation. Keep source explanations separate from learner performance observations.
- [ ] Show objective coverage, attempts and practice feedback without presenting them as proof
  of exam readiness or awarding a qualification. Formal examination controls are outside scope.
- [x] When underlying evidence changes or access is revoked, flag affected activities for review
  and prevent publication or delivery that would disclose the removed evidence.
- [ ] Teacher-validated drafts generated from certified passages (the former WIKI-401 wiki idea)
  use this same draft and review flow.

## Frontend and design

Provide a preparation flow: choose objective and sources, draft, inspect evidence, edit, preview
as learner, publish. Reuse rich answer blocks and source readers. A shared lesson proposal created
from a cross-subject comparison remains a draft until the responsible teachers review it.

Keep optional competency information out of simple personal-study flows. Show who reviewed an
activity and which source revisions it uses. Place practice results in the learner's course view,
with teacher access limited to the relevant course.

## Acceptance

- [x] A trainer can publish a reviewed exercise linked to an objective and exact sources.
- [x] Learners cannot retrieve drafts or answer keys before the configured feedback step.
- [x] A source revision change flags dependent activities without silently rewriting past attempts.
- [x] Personal activities remain private and are not labelled as independently reviewed.
- [ ] A multi-subject lesson preserves each source's attribution and separates proposals from facts.

## Livré

Première tranche : l'exercice à réponse courte. Les règles sont dans
[architecture](../../docs/architecture.md#exercices-sourcés), les libellés dans
[vocabulaire](../../docs/vocabulaire.md).

- Objectifs par cours, référence libre (`migrations/0019_activites.sql`). La page de préparation la
  masque en mode personnel.
- Génération en un appel (`src/core/activite.ts`) : chaque champ est vérifié par `verifier()`, un
  champ mal ancré est rejeté et compté. FIC-101 et REVI-101 renvoient à ce mécanisme pour ne pas
  en écrire un second.
- Brouillon, relu, publié, à revoir, avec l'historique de relecture par version. La préparation et
  les exercices vivent sous l'onglet « Exercices » de la bibliothèque, sans entrée de navigation de
  plus. L'élève les trouve dans ses ressources.
- Le corrigé n'est rendu qu'avec une tentative du compte sur la version actuelle. L'élève coche
  les critères qu'il pense remplir.
- `statuer()`, `supprimerSource()` et les changements de lecteurs ou de cours d'une source
  signalent les exercices à revoir. La suppression retire aussi les passages copiés.

Hors de cette tranche :

- L'explication d'étude, les procédures pratiques et les grilles d'analyse de cas.
- Le suivi des objectifs, et la lecture des tentatives par l'enseignant du cours.
- Les propositions de séance issues d'une comparaison de matières, et les brouillons de wiki.
- Le retour noté par un modèle, et l'extraction de la fiche de chapitre (FIC-102).
- Une évaluation de la consigne de génération : elle demande un jeu d'exercices annotés.
