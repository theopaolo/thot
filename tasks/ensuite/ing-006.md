## Notes

Une réingestion réécrit `document.txt` en place. Les offsets des citations déjà données aux
élèves pointent alors vers un autre texte ([[ing-005]] le provoquera). Écrire chaque
extraction dans une révision et garder la révision citée par un ancien message.

## Extension for EVO-001

This is the shared prerequisite for richer extraction, corrected transcripts and durable citations.
Keep one implementation of revisions for [DOCX](ing-109.md), [PPTX](ing-110.md),
[media](med-101.md) and [structured answers](ans-101.md).

### Work

- [x] Store an immutable extraction revision with canonical text, blocks and extraction warnings.
  A source identifies the resource, while its selected revision identifies the content currently
  available for new answers.
- [ ] Add an images/asset manifest and parser provenance to each revision. Les images restent
  rattachées à la source, et aucun extracteur n'a encore de version à enregistrer.
- [ ] Extend locators to pages, slides, paragraphs/table cells and time intervals as required by
  each format. Preserve the existing Unicode citation invariant. Rendering must not reconstruct
  offsets from differently formatted display text. Pages et paragraphes existent par les blocs.
  Une diapositive porte son numéro dans `page` (ING-110), une rangée de tableau Word garde ses
  cellules sur une ligne (ING-109). Intervalles de temps : MED-101.
- [ ] Associate images with their real blocks/slides and distinguish embedded captions, generated
  descriptions and reviewed descriptions. Do not attach every image to the first text chunk.
  Une image Word ou PowerPoint garde sa section, et sa légende accompagne le premier passage de
  cette section. Les images PDF et HTML restent sur le premier passage.
- [x] Pin citations to their evidence revision. Keep legacy responses readable by assigning their
  original canonical content a revision.
- [ ] Pin reviewed generated descriptions and learning activities to their evidence revision. Une
  légende relue suit les octets de son image, et aucune activité n'existe encore.
- [x] Write new extraction output into a staging revision, verify it and then select it atomically.
  Review does not carry over silently when the evidence text or generated descriptions change.
- [x] Keep old citations readable only while current access permits it. A retained revision is
  not a way around revocation.
- [ ] Apply deletion and retention rules to retained revisions, with SRC-002.
