## Product rules

Use a small fixed set of capabilities: administer the instance, manage designated teaching
materials, review/publish them, and learn from accessible materials. One account may combine
these capabilities. Course membership bounds teaching and learner access. Avoid a user-defined
role builder in this increment.

A source can be private to its owner, shared with authorised colleagues, or published to selected
learner groups. Its extraction/review state and eligibility as answer evidence are independent
from its audience. Personal selection is labelled as such and does not imply expert certification.
A course affiliation alone never expands the source's audience. Publication and explicit sharing
grants determine access, together with the reader's membership.

## Backend

- [ ] Associate ownership with account identifiers. Preserve historical attribution text when an
  existing source's named teacher cannot be mapped unambiguously to an account.
- [ ] Migrate existing teacher and learner capabilities without widening source or conversation
  access. Bootstrap administrator authority explicitly rather than granting it to every teacher.
- [ ] Centralise source eligibility and access checks. Apply them before retrieval/reranking and
  on readers, originals, images, transcripts, media ranges, suggestions, jobs and citations.
- [ ] Allow approved teacher-only sources to participate in authorised teacher searches. Preserve
  the distinction between being readable and being eligible to support generated answers.
- [ ] Recheck access before publishing an asynchronous result and when reopening one. Revocation
  invalidates search results, derived previews and accessible answer content.
- [ ] Preserve private teacher and solo conversations. Constrain learner-question review to the
  relevant course authority. Make that visibility clear to learners before they ask questions.
- [ ] Apply ownership and course permissions to changes and bulk review, not only to reading.

## Interface

Use a compact audience control on resources and bulk review. Show who reviewed the resource and
which revision they reviewed. Distinguish available for my study, shared with colleagues and
published to learners. Do not use qualification language for source review.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Test owner, colleague, unrelated teacher, enrolled learner, unrelated learner and personal
  owner across reads, writes, retrieval, citations and generated previews.
- [ ] Copying a citation, image URL or media range request cannot bypass the source policy.
- [ ] Revoking a source during generation cannot publish its text to the now-unauthorised reader.
- [ ] Sharing a source does not share its uploader's conversations or answer keys.
