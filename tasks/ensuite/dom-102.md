## Notes

`garderProfil` in [domaines.ts](../../src/app/domaines.ts) compared a profile change with every
other subject. Two subjects created with the wrong profile blocked each other: changing either one
conflicted with the other, even in an empty instance. The catalogue also had no rename or delete,
so migration 0007 left the pilot with a course named after its subject.

## Work

- [x] Change the profile of every subject in one action while the instance has no resource, in
  the administration page and the CLI.
- [x] Rename subjects, courses and topics. Identifiers and affiliations stay the same.
- [x] Delete a subject, course or topic that no source, affiliation or message references. A used
  entry stays, and archiving it belongs to [CFG-101](cfg-101.md).

## Acceptance

- [x] Two arts subjects in an empty instance switch to the general profile in one action.
- [x] The same action is refused once a resource exists.
- [x] Deleting a used entry is refused with the reason. A deleted entry leaves the filters and
  upload forms.
- [x] Renaming the pilot course and one of its topics keeps their affiliations and the existing
  learner pages.

## Delivered

`changerProfil` sets the profile of every subject in one action, from the administration page or
`thot profil <general|arts-appliques>`. It is refused once a resource has another profile.
`renommer` and `supprimer` work on subjects, courses and topics, in the page and the CLI. Deleting
a subject or a course also removes its courses and topics, in one transaction. A resource, an
affiliation or a learner question that uses any of them blocks the whole deletion, and the error
names the entry. Questions stop blocking once `THOT_RETENTION_JOURS` purges them.

Renaming keeps identifiers, so affiliations and learner pages follow the new name. Two imports
still look things up by name: `--matiere=arts-appliques` finds the subject by its original label,
and a séquence without a course goes to the topic with the same name. After a rename they recreate
the old name. `docs/exploitation.md` points to the `--matiere-id`, `--cours-id` and `--theme-id`
options instead.
